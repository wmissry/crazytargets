How a round goes

  • Everyone gets a corner and a colour. Two, three or four players on one iPad.
  • Touch your ball inside your corner and flick. It travels as fast as your finger.
  • Balls never run out, and a ball that goes in scores for its colour, even if someone else knocked it in.
  • Rounds last one, two or three minutes. Highest score wins.
  • Tournament mode strings three stages together and crowns one champion.
  • No accounts and no internet needed to play. In English and Arabic.
