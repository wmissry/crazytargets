Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026

Crazy Targets is a game for iPad made by Wayle Missry. The short version: the game does not collect any information about you.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and it does not send anything about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPad

The game saves a few settings on your device so they are there next time: the language, the round length, the sound levels and any player names you type. They never leave your iPad, and they are removed when you delete the game.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple only tells the game whether the unlock is owned, including through Family Sharing, so it can open the extra stages. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Crazy Targets is suitable for families. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

This website

These pages use no cookies and no analytics. If you switch the home page to Arabic, that choice is remembered in your own browser and nowhere else. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address to run and protect its service, as described in the GitHub privacy statement.

If you write to us

When you email us we receive your address and your message, and we use them only to reply to you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
