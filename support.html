Help with Crazy Targets for iPad.

Can't find your answer below? Write to crazytargets.app@gmail.com and tell us which iPad you have and what happened. We reply in English or Arabic.

How do we play?

Put the iPad flat on a table and sit one player at each corner. Touch your ball inside your coloured corner and flick it toward the target. The ball travels as fast as your finger moved, so the right power matters more than a hard shot.

A new ball appears in your corner whenever you need one. When a ball goes in, the point goes to the player of that colour, even if someone else knocked it in. Each stage shows a short card with its own rules before the round starts.

How many people can play, and on which devices?

Two, three or four players share one iPad. The game is made for iPad only.

What is free, and what does the purchase unlock?

Spinning Hole and Penguin Basket are free. A single one-time purchase unlocks the other six stages and tournament mode. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

I bought the unlock but the stages are still locked

On the home screen tap Unlock all stages, then tap Restore purchases at the bottom of that window. Make sure the iPad is signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase and is connected to the internet.

Does the purchase work with Family Sharing?

Yes. When purchase sharing is turned on in your family group, one purchase unlocks the game for every family member. If a family member still sees locked stages, they can tap Unlock all stages and then Restore purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change the language, the round length or the player names?

Open Settings on the home screen. You can switch between English and Arabic, choose rounds of one, two or three minutes, and type a name for each colour. The game starts in Arabic on an iPad set to Arabic, and in English otherwise.

Children keep swiping out of the game

During a round the game already asks for two swipes before the iPad leaves it. For a stronger lock use Guided Access: in the iPad's Settings open Accessibility, then Guided Access, turn it on and set a passcode. In the game, triple-click the top button and tap Start. The Steady play page on the home screen has the same steps.

There is no sound

Check the iPad's volume and that it is not muted, because the game follows the iPad's silent setting. Then open Settings in the game, or the pause menu, and check the music and sound sliders and the Mute all switch.
